What website problems quietly cost calls?

You do not need a flashy redesign to lose jobs. Small friction on a phone is enough for someone to close the tab and call the next shop on Google.

Issues I see most often

  • No clear click-to-call. Phone number is an image, or buried in a footer.
  • Contact form broken or unused. Submissions go nowhere, or the form asks for too much.
  • Slow mobile load. Huge photos and old builders make the page feel stuck.
  • Services page is vague. Visitors cannot tell if you handle their job or city.
  • HTTP or security warnings. Browsers scare people off before they read a word.
  • Hours or address wrong. Details disagree with your Google listing.

What "good enough" looks like

On a phone, a stranger should find your phone number in a few seconds, see what you do, and trust that you are open. That is the bar. Fancy animations are optional.

Optimization vs redesign

Many Downriver businesses already have a site on WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, or a builder their cousin set up years ago. Often the fix is updates and cleanup, not starting over. Full redesigns cost more time and money than most single-location shops need in year one.
