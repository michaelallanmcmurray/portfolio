What does the free listing audit cover?

The free audit is a short, written review of your Google listing and website. You get three specific quick wins. No jargon dump, and no obligation to hire anyone.

Google listing

  • Primary category and whether it fits the work you actually sell
  • Hours, phone, address, and service area accuracy
  • Photo count and how recently the profile was updated
  • Last post date (if any)
  • Recent reviews and whether anyone is replying

Website

  • Mobile usability and whether the phone number is easy to tap
  • Contact form presence and obvious breakage
  • HTTPS / security warnings
  • Obvious broken links or stale details that disagree with Google

Quick search check

I search your business name plus city and note who shows up in the map results. That gives a baseline before any monthly work.

What you receive

A short note with three concrete fixes you could do yourself or hand to someone else. If monthly retainer help makes sense later, we can talk. The audit itself stands alone.
